Interpretation

The portfolio is concentrated in surface hydrology, hydrometeorology, statistical hydrology, uncertainty analysis, and hydroinformatics. Water-resources applications are present as a secondary dimension, while river hydraulics is peripheral in the current set of studies.

Hydrogeology and water quality are absent from the represented articles. The radar therefore describes the actual thematic coverage of the portfolio rather than an intended or normative disciplinary profile.

How to read the scores

The 0–10 values are article-level descriptors. A value close to 10 indicates that a domain is central to the study; intermediate values indicate a substantive but secondary role; values close to 0 indicate that the domain is peripheral or absent. The aggregate radar is calculated directly from these article-level values.
